What is GunksFest?

GunksFest is the annual festival of the Gunks Climbers Coalition — an education-first gathering in the Shawangunks built around climbing clinics and workshops led by experienced local guides.

Clinics & Workshops

Self-rescue, anchor building, lead climbing, multipitch strategy, and more — taught by seasoned Gunks guides.

Vendors & Food

Gear makers, local makers, and food vendors on site throughout the weekend.

Evening Film Festival

Climbing films under the stars, plus special guests to get the stoke going.

Camping

New for 2026 — camp on site and make a full weekend of it.

Presented in Partnership

GunksFest is organized by the Gunks Climbers Coalition, in partnership with the Mohonk Preserve.

A New Home for 2026

This year the festival spreads across two iconic Shawangunks locations.

Ulster County Fairground

Basecamp

Camping, vendors, and the evening film festival — the heart of the weekend.

Mohonk Preserve

On the Rock

Daytime climbing clinics and workshops on the legendary Gunks cliffs.
